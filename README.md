## Student Profile and Photo Upload (Kritima)

- Students can view and edit their profile.
- Students can update their personal information.
- Students can upload a profile photo from the gallery.
- Supported photo formats: JPG and PNG.
- Maximum photo size: 5 MB.
- Student profile form validation is covered by tests.
